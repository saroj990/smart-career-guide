# Day 6 — Decision tree + random forest

## Goal
Compare **single tree** vs **ensemble** (Random Forest) to logistic regression.

## Key ideas
- **Tree:** splits on features; prone to **overfitting**.
- **Random Forest:** many trees + bagging → often better generalization.
- **Feature importance:** which inputs matter most (global, model-specific).

## Checkpoint
Why can an ensemble generalize better than one deep tree?
